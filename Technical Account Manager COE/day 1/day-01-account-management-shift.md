## Key learnings

### Outcomes over adoption
In a services consultancy, success is measured by whether the engineering we deliver translates into meaningful business outcomes for the client, not by whether the client uses a product.

### The Independent Pod model
Incubyte's pods are cross-functional and autonomous. The Account Manager's role is alignment, removing blockers and helping the client and the pod operate as one team.

### Consultative account growth
Growth comes from understanding the client's business landscape and proactively proposing solutions, rather than from renewals, upselling and cross-selling.

### Quality with pragmatism
Quality includes engineering craftsmanship, technical decisions and long-term sustainability, balanced against delivering meaningful value incrementally.

## Exercises

### Exercise 1: Three things that change

**1. From Product Adoption to Business and Delivery Outcomes**

In my previous role, a significant part of my responsibility was driving product adoption, engagement, retention and expansion. At Incubyte, the focus shifts towards understanding the client's larger business objectives and ensuring that the engineering solutions we deliver translate into meaningful business outcomes. Success is not just about whether the client uses a product, but whether we are solving the right problems for them.

**2. From Coordinating Teams to Enabling an Autonomous Partnership**

Previously, I worked closely with internal Product, Support and Sales teams to address customer requirements and drive resolutions. In a services consultancy, particularly with Incubyte's Independent Pod model, I need to work more closely with cross-functional engineering teams, enable their autonomy, and create an environment where the client and the pod operate as one team. My role becomes more about alignment, removing blockers and enabling teams to make informed decisions.

**3. From Managing Existing Expectations to Proactively Creating Opportunities**

In SaaS Customer Success, a lot of account growth comes through adoption, renewals, upselling and cross-selling. At Incubyte, I see Account Management becoming much more consultative. It means understanding the client's business landscape, asking the right questions, identifying problems they may not have articulated yet, and working with the team to proactively propose solutions rather than simply responding to requests.

### Exercise 2: The value that will stretch me most

**Relentless Pursuit of Quality with Pragmatism**

I think this value will stretch me the most because my previous experience has largely been in a SaaS environment, where I have focused on customer outcomes through product adoption, engagement, retention and relationship management.

Moving into a software services consultancy introduces another dimension of quality for me to understand: the quality of engineering, craftsmanship, technical decisions and the long-term sustainability of what we deliver.

I will need to develop a deeper understanding of how engineering teams approach quality, make technical trade-offs and balance speed with sustainable delivery. I also want to get better at connecting these technical decisions to the value they create for the client.

What makes this particularly interesting is that quality cannot come at the cost of pragmatism. It is about delivering meaningful value incrementally while keeping the bigger picture in mind.

## Reflection
I'm not starting over in Account Management. I'm building on my customer success experience while learning to operate in a more consultative, engineering-driven environment. The biggest shift is moving from measuring product adoption to measuring business outcomes, and from coordinating internal teams to enabling an autonomous pod. I see this as an opportunity to expand my perspective beyond customer and account management into understanding the craft behind the solutions we deliver.
